2 · Hierarchy — what nests inside what
Three levels deep, maximum. A customer holds accounts; each account issues agent cards; each card carries one policy; each policy produces decisions; each decision holds its evidence. Depth beyond level 3 is a detail sheet, never a new tab.

Two tracks. The setup track is user-initiated and linear. The decision track is agent-initiated and interrupt-driven — it can start at any moment and always terminates in the log.
